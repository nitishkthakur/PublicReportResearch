Citigroup Q1 2025 Earnings Report

Key Metrics, Findings & Insights

Key Financial Highlights

  • Net Income: $4.1B ($1.96 EPS), up 3% YoY
  • Revenue: $21.6B, slight YoY increase
  • ROCE: 13.4% (up from 8.0% in prior quarter)
  • Return to Shareholders: $2.8B via dividends/buybacks (74% payout ratio)
  • Book Value per Share: $103.90 (up 5% YoY)
  • CET1 Capital Ratio: 13.4% (down slightly from 13.6%)

Segment Performance

  • Services: $4.9B revenue (+3%), net income up 7% to $1.6B
  • Markets: $6.0B revenue (+12%), net income up 29% to $1.8B
  • Banking: $2.0B revenue (+12%), net income up 53% to $543M
  • Wealth: $2.1B revenue (+24%), net income down 15% to $284M
  • USPB: $5.2B revenue (+2%), net income up 115% to $745M
  • Legacy Franchises: $1.6B revenue (-11%) due to wind-downs and currency impacts

Capital & Risk Metrics

  • Tangible Common Equity (TCE): $170.9B (up 3.2% YoY)
  • Cost of Credit: $348M (down 12% YoY), but $2.7B in credit costs (+15% YoY)
  • Non-accrual Loans: $2.7B (-2% YoY)
  • SLR: Stable at 5.8%
  • All Other Net Loss: $885M (up 20% QoQ)

Actionable Insights

  • **Markets & Banking Growth:** Strong performance in Markets (12% revenue growth) and Banking (12% revenue growth) driven by Fixed Income, Equity derivatives, and Advisory fees. Sustain momentum in these areas.
  • **Credit Cost Management:** Elevated credit costs in Wealth (-$98M) and Legacy Franchises ($359M) signal risk exposure. Enhance credit risk modeling and reserve adequacy.
  • **Cost Efficiency:** Operating expenses down 5% YoY to $13.4B, but $34M in divestiture-related costs. Focus on cost control in wind-down operations (e.g., Mexico, Asia).
  • **Wealth Segment Diversification:** Wealth revenue up 24% but net income down 15%. Explore fee-based income streams to offset credit risk volatility.
  • **Capital Allocation:** CET1 ratio stable at 13.4%, but $885M All Other loss impacts capital. Review non-GAAP measures and adjust capital deployment strategy.

Key Drivers & Outlook

  • **Positive Momentum:** Services revenue at decade-high, Markets RoTCE at 14.3%, and USPB net income surge (+115%) reflect strong operational execution.
  • **Challenges:** Legacy Franchises (-11% revenue), macroeconomic risks in Wealth, and All Other losses ($885M) require strategic repositioning.
  • **Opportunities:** Cross-border transaction growth (+5%), U.S. dollar clearing (+8%), and deposit spreads in Wealth offer near-term growth levers.
